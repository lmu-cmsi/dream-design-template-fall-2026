## Overview

- Give an overarching paragraph or two about what you are designing and what gap you are trying to fill with it.

### Goals

- List out directly what you are trying to achieve with your design.

### Milestones

- List out the accomplishments you have to make, the order you have to make them in, and when you have to make them to finish your design on time and deliver the design you want to deliver.

## Existing designs

- Talk about alternatives to your design that already exist

## Proposed design

- Talk through your envisioned design. What sets it apart from the existing ones? What are you trying to achieve?

## Open questions/room for improvements

- What can you not determine yet, that might have to wait for prototyping to be figured out?
- Set a realistic goal of what you can accomplish in the time you are given. Then think about what you'd like to do in the future after you accomplish that.
